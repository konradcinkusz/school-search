# Portal requirements: demand, required content, compliance and funding (Poland)

Date: 2026-10-14. Author: research agent. Status: input for owner decisions, not legal advice.

## Method and limits (read first)

- Evidence came from web search result summaries only. **Direct page fetches (WebFetch, curl) were blocked by the environment's egress policy** (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` / proxy 403 for gov.pl, eur-lex, eurydice, prawo.pl, stat.gov.pl). No primary page text was read in full by the researcher. Therefore **no claim here is VERIFIED against the primary text**. Confidence labels:
  - **REPORTED**: stated by a search-result summary of an identifiable publisher, consistent with the repo or with other sources, but not read at the primary URL.
  - **UNVERIFIED**: weak, secondary, old, or contradictory source; or inferred.
  - **NOT FOUND**: searched, nothing usable.
- Per AGENTS.md no competitor portal was fetched or searched for; funding comparables (section D) are non-Polish directories found by a neutral query and cited only for their self-described models.

## Summary: 10 key findings

1. **Preschool participation is near-universal; the choice problem is which facility, not whether.** 97.1% of children aged 3-6 took part in preschool education on 30 Sep 2024; 22.3k preschool-education institutions (14k preschools), almost 1.5m children, school year 2024/25 (GUS "Oswiata i wychowanie 2024/2025", 28 Oct 2025, via prawo.pl and Sejm print 2803). Confidence: REPORTED.
2. **Primary entrants are falling.** MEN forecast for 2025/26: 396.6k pupils in grade 1 (over 30k fewer than the year before) and 3,236.9k in primary schools overall. It is a forecast, not a count. Confidence: REPORTED (forecast; actual SIO/GUS count not found).
3. **Nurseries are the scarce, fragmented level.** End of 2025: over 5k nurseries, 1k children's clubs, 2.4k day carers, 256.2k places; 163.5k children in nurseries, -1.4% vs 2024 (GUS, via Infor.pl). Places per 1,000 children under 3 range from 49.2 (Mazowieckie) to 2.4 (Opolskie) (same source; it mixes sources, see caveat). Confidence: REPORTED / UNVERIFIED for the regional spread.
4. **Non-public nurseries dominate and cost more.** NIK (2020-22): non-public nurseries about 80-82% of all; fees up to 800 PLN per month higher than municipal nurseries, and cost was the main reason for lower place use (NIK report via press summary). Eurydice: municipal nurseries 25.8% of all in 2023. Confidence: REPORTED. Implication: **fees are the top-value fact for level 1.**
5. **Public money is expanding nursery capacity (Aktywny Maluch, ex Maluch+).** Programme target over 102k new places by 2029, about 6.5 bn PLN from FERS, KPO and state budget; the 2026 call closed 31 Jan 2026 and no next call was announced at the time of the source (jakiwniosek.pl, a secondary site; the programme document is on poznan.uw.gov.pl). Confidence: REPORTED / UNVERIFIED for call status.
6. **Admissions are local, not national.** Dates and criteria are set per gmina (resolution of the council plus an order of the wojt/mayor), under the Prawo oswiatowe of 14 Dec 2016. Example 2026/27: Warsaw registration 10-24 Feb, lists 30 Mar; Zlawies Wielka 16-27 Feb, lists 13 Mar. Children living in a primary school's catchment are admitted on notification. Confidence: REPORTED. Implication: a national calendar cannot be shown; per-gmina data with source and as-of is required.
7. **No Polish national survey of how parents choose a school was found.** Only a 2011 pilot in one Elblag school (e-mentor), guides, and a 2026 private-school survey announcement (Our Kids). Foreign studies (OECD 2015 and others) put safety, teaching quality and location at the top. Confidence: UNVERIFIED for Poland. This is a demand-evidence gap the owner should fill with their own parent interviews or a survey.
8. **Curriculum reform changes what "a school" offers from 1 Sep 2026.** New core curriculum in preschool and grades 1 and 4 (Reforma26 Kompas Jutra); the president vetoed the enabling Prawo oswiatowe amendment in Dec 2025 and the ministry said it would proceed by regulation; status on 30 Jun 2026 was a bill in the Sejm (portaloswiatowy.pl, gov.pl). Confidence: REPORTED, legal status UNVERIFIED.
9. **Compliance load for a free review portal is real but scaled by size.** DSA art. 19 removes the online-platform section 3 duties for micro and small enterprises (art. 24(3) excepted) and art. 15(2) removes the annual transparency report duty for them, but notice-and-action, statement of reasons, contact points and terms apply to hosting providers regardless (art. 11, 12, 14, 16, 17). Omnibus rules make it a blacklisted practice to present reviews as from real consumers without reasonable verification steps. Confidence: REPORTED; applicability to this owner (entity size, "online platform" status) is a lawyer item.
10. **Sponsored placement is permitted if labelled and kept out of ranking; the repo already enforces the hard part.** UCPD art. 7 / Polish unfair-practices act treat undisclosed paid content as misleading; ranking parameters and paid ranking must be disclosed (Art. 7(4a) UCPD). Comparable school directories sell premium profiles or banners and state publicly that this does not affect rankings (Compare Private Schools AU, IB-Schools UK). Confidence: REPORTED.

### A2. How parents choose

- Poland-specific evidence is thin. Elblag pilot (2011, one school, author calls it non-generalisable) on school image: http://www.e-mentor.edu.pl/artykul/index/numer/55/id/1105 (UNVERIFIED relevance).
- Guides (Rodzice.pl, Compass) list location, class size, extracurriculars, after-school care as key; they are advice, not research (UNVERIFIED).
- A Warsaw report on opinions about nurseries (2025, municipal) found parents choose by trust in staff and playground availability, with elimination diets least important (https://zlobki.waw.pl/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Raport-z-badania-opinii-o-warszawskich-instytucjach-opieki-nad-dziecmi-w-wieku-do-lat-3-%E2%80%93-2025.pdf). REPORTED, one city.
- A Piotrkow preschool parent survey: safety and school readiness most cited; some parents wanted more frequent progress information (single institution, UNVERIFIED as general).
- Foreign comparators: OECD 2015 (parents value academics but worry more about safety, environment, reputation); a California survey of 1,023 parents (safety, special-needs programmes, staff); FFT Education Datalab UK (distance equally important for both parents; safety 67% vs 60% mothers vs fathers). Used only as directional evidence; not Polish. UNVERIFIED for Poland.
- Municipal tools already exist: Warsaw's preschool map filters by meals, location and infrastructure, with a short card and a PDF per facility (https://warszawa19115.pl/-/przedszkola-pytania). This is a government source showing the information type that cities themselves consider useful.

What parents say they miss: **NOT FOUND** as a national survey. Candidates inferred from the sources (all UNVERIFIED): fees and what they include (NIK 2011 found 8 of 10 institutions charged tuition and none specified what it covered), real vacancies, progress communication, and honest cross-gmina comparison.

### A3. Pain points

- Vacancies: NIK 2019 control: applications exceeded places by 30-60% in nearly half of the audited gminas; nearly one child in three did not get the chosen preschool, mostly 3- and 4-year-olds (via prawo.pl, https://www.prawo.pl/samorzad/dostepnosc-przedszkoli-raport-nik-z-2019-r,435700.html). REPORTED but old; current position since the demographic fall is UNVERIFIED.
- Fees: NIK nursery audit (2020-22) as in finding 4. Preschool fee legality (free core curriculum hours, paid extra hours) is a fee-transparency issue (NIK 2011, via press, UNVERIFIED).
- Timelines: per-gmina, compressed into Feb-Mar (finding 6). A parent in two gminas must read two schedules.
- Nursery shortage: addressed by Aktywny Maluch (finding 5); NIK recommended the minister of family increase territorial availability, linking it to the 2022 EU Barcelona targets. REPORTED.

### A4. Reform context

- School-year 2026/27: new core curriculum in preschool, grades 1 and 4 (finding 8). Health education and civic education entered schools 1 Sep 2025 (PAP / portaloswiatowy). REPORTED.
- A compulsory preschool year for 5-year-olds exists in law (Prawo oswiatowe); no change found in this research. Not separately searched: UNVERIFIED that no 2026 change applies.
- School-starting age: not researched. NOT VERIFIED.

## C. Legal and compliance checklist (flags for a lawyer; not legal advice)

Legend: [x] already in repo per docs; [ ] open; (L) lawyer.

**GDPR**
- [ ] Controller identity named in privacy notice (repo: provider identity not supplied, `docs/legal/DSA.md` section 3) (L).
- [ ] Lawful basis per processing purpose recorded (legitimate interest for reviews and moderation is plausible, consent for newsletters); repo drafts exist, basis wording to be approved (L). Source basis: GDPR art. 6.
- [ ] DPIA screening for reviews and for AI-assisted triage: UODO list of operations needing a DPIA (Monitor Polski 2019; EDPB criticised the first version; UODO communication "Kiedy trzeba przeprowadzic ocene skutkow" of 31 Mar 2025, reported by Wolters Kluwer https://www.wolterskluwer.com/pl-pl/expert-insights/ocena-dpia-dla-systemow-ai). Whether a school-review portal triggers it was not determined. UNVERIFIED (L).
- [ ] Children's data: the portal addresses parents, not children; art. 8 GDPR age of consent for information-society services directed at a child is 16 in Poland (money.pl 2018, UNVERIFIED currency). Keep the service adult-only, state it in the terms; ADR-0021's secondary-student edition would change this.
- [ ] Reviews must not contain data of a named child or teacher; repo has no teacher or pupil entity. Moderation rule needed (L).
- [ ] Retention for AI prompts (open item O1 in UI-UX backlog).

**DSA (Regulation 2022/2065)**
- [x] Contact points (art. 11, 12): endpoint built, address owner-supplied.
- [x] Terms (art. 14), notice-and-action (art. 16), statement of reasons (art. 17), internal complaints (art. 20): implemented per `docs/legal/DSA.md`.
- [ ] Transparency report (art. 15): exemption for micro and small enterprises in art. 15(2) (Standardy Prawa art. text, https://standardyprawa.pl/akt/573/art/65651 summary) if the owner qualifies under Recommendation 2003/361/EC (L).
- [ ] Online-platform section (art. 19 excludes micro/small except art. 24(3)): confirm entity size and whether reviews make this an "online platform" (L). Art. 24(3) requires publishing average monthly active recipients: confirm.
- [ ] Art. 26 (advertising labelling and who paid) and art. 25 (dark patterns) apply to online platforms; consistent with the sponsored label design, regardless of exemption.
- [ ] Out-of-court dispute settlement (art. 21) is not implemented; applicability and exemption (L).

**Consumer law (reviews and sponsored content)**
- [ ] Omnibus directive 2019/2161: a trader giving access to reviews must say whether and how it checks them; claiming reviews are from real users without reasonable steps is a blacklisted practice; commissioning fake reviews is blacklisted. Sources: Commission notice OJ C 526 (2021) as discussed in law-firm summaries (e.g. https://www.addleshawgoddard.com/en/insights/insights-briefings/2020/competition/the-eu-omnibus-directive--time-to-prepare-for-strengthened-consumer-laws/); primary text not read. Repo labels reviewers "Rodzic/opiekun (niezweryfikowany)": consistent, but the **verification statement must appear next to the reviews** (L).
- [ ] UOKiK has fined review sellers and said platforms may be liable for not protecting consumers from fake reviews (press summaries, wiadomoscikosmetyczne.pl 2023, dates uncertain). REPORTED.
- [ ] Sponsored content: undisclosed paid editorial content is an unfair commercial practice (Polish act on counteracting unfair market practices art. 7 pkt 11? exact article not confirmed: UNVERIFIED). A final UOKiK influencer guide was in preparation in 2022; whether it was published was not confirmed. Use: label "Tresc sponsorowana" (repo already does).
- [ ] Ranking transparency: if any ordering shows paid results, disclose main parameters (UCPD art. 7(4a)). Repo sells no ordering effect; keep the methodology page explicit.
- [ ] Sale terms, withdrawal right, VAT for school customers (B2B): OPEN in `SALES-TERMS-DRAFT.pl.md` (L).

**ePrivacy / cookies**
- [ ] Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej of 12 Jul 2024 (Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1221) art. 399: storage or access on the terminal only with clear prior information and consent, except strictly necessary cookies; UKE supervises (arslege.pl / standardyprawa.pl summaries). Current site uses only a necessary session cookie, so no banner is needed *as long as no analytics or maps cookies are added*; an embedded map provider and any analytics must be checked (L). Confidence REPORTED.

**Accessibility**
- Polish act of 26 Apr 2024 (Dz.U. 2024 poz. 731) implementing the European Accessibility Act, in force from 28 Jun 2025; microenterprises providing services are excluded (gov.pl / Ministry of Funds and Regional Policy, https://www.gov.pl/web/dostepnosc-cyfrowa/polski-akt-o-dostepnosci--uslugi-handlu-elektronicznego). The scope is defined by listed services (notably e-commerce); whether a **free informational portal** is a covered service is UNVERIFIED; the sponsored-profile sales flow may be. (L)
- The act on digital accessibility of public entities' websites (2019) binds public bodies, not a private portal: standard knowledge, not re-verified here. UNVERIFIED.
- Regardless of legal duty, WCAG 2.1 AA is the reasonable bar, and the repo's backlog row 9 (axe in CI) is the matching task.

**Publication of school results**
- No restriction on publishing school-level exam aggregates was found, but none was specifically searched in the law. The repo already applies a cohort threshold (ADR-0009) as a privacy and noise rule, not as a statutory one. UNVERIFIED; ask the lawyer and re-read the CKE/OKE terms of use (SOURCES §3.3).

**Reuse of public sector information**
- Act of 11 Aug 2021 on open data and reuse of public sector information (Dz.U. 2021 poz. 1641; consolidated text cited as Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1524): defines dynamic data and high-value datasets and requires open formats. Terms of fees and licence were not obtained. The repo records SIO as CC0 per brief and every other licence as OPEN. REPORTED / UNVERIFIED for fees.
